# Release v2.9.0 — Environment Gate Consolidation & DTE Integration

This release resolves the 10 critical structural and logic defects in the Environment Gate as outlined in the `ifix_plan.md` review. It introduces bucket scoring, dynamic `dte` injection, and full session-aware gating.

## 🏗️ Structural & Architecture Fixes
- **Bucket Scoring (Issue 1 & 3):** Resolved the collinearity bug (C2+C3+C6) where multiple momentum signals fired on a single market event. `get_environment_score()` now groups the 10 conditions into Structure, Momentum, and Context buckets. A "GO" verdict requires $\ge 1$ point from all three buckets, preventing a verdict driven entirely by correlated momentum.
- **Dealer O'Clock Regime Switch (Issue 2):** When `dealer_oc` is active and `dte <= 1`, the gate applies a regime switch: C5 (IV Cheap) and C7 (Term Structure) are deactivated, C9 (VEX alignment) is double-weighted, and the C10 penalty is inverted to a bonus, correctly reflecting the increased value of charm flow near expiry.
- **Dynamic DTE Injection (Issue 5):** Re-architected all callers of `get_environment_score()` (`recommender.py`, `scheduler.py`, `tracker.py`, `ws.py`, `market.py`) to fetch and compute current `dte` (Days to Expiry) dynamically and pass it to the gate, unlocking the Dealer O'Clock regime switch.

## 🛡️ Gating & Session Defences
- **Volume Guard (Issue 4):** Added a hard-cap blocking the gate verdict at "WAIT" if the intraday snap volume is $\le 30\%$ of the rolling 20-day average, preventing false positive GO verdicts on illiquid pre-market or holiday sessions.
- **Opening Turbulence Block (Issue 6):** Created a new `MarketSession.OPENING_TURBULENCE` bucket. The highly volatile post-auction window (09:15–09:30) is now strictly blocked as "NO_GO".
- **Term Structure Penalty (Issue 7):** Refactored C7 logic. Instead of a default $+1$ bonus, `BACKWARDATION` is now correctly scored as a $-1$ penalty. A missing IV shape returns $0$ pts rather than artificially inflating the gate score.

## ⚙️ Config & Normalization
- **Max Score Segregation (Issue 10):** The scoring aggregation now safely separates bonus and penalty points, allowing the engine to handle sub-zero condition contributions without incorrectly triggering the `GATE_MAX_SCORE` validation.
- **Static IVP Threshold (Issue 9):** The normal VIX regime branch of C5 no longer relies on a hardcoded $50.0$ threshold and instead uses the tunable parameter `VIX_NORMAL_IVP_THRESHOLD`.
- **S_score Recalibration (Issue 8):** Temporarily reverted the S_score star rating thresholds back to the 150-scale values ($100.0, 80.0, 60.0$) in `config.py` to account for the missing `W_MOMENTUM` factor. The factor will fully reactivate once `avg_volume_20d` is implemented in the data pipeline.
