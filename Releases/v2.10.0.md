# OptDash v2.10.0 (AI Recommender & Screener Pipeline Fixes)

This release implements 10 high-priority system reliability fixes across the `pre_flight`, `recommender`, `screener`, `quality`, and `narrative` engines, resolving edge cases related to DTE calculations, dynamic targets, and screener scoring.

## Critical Engine Additions
- **DTE-Aware Theta Guard (Issue #1)**: Replaced a single static Theta ratio guard with a DTE-scaled model, dynamically applying `PREFLIGHT_THETA_RATIO_DTE12 = 0.08` to expressly permit DTE 1 and 2 trading without silent rejection due to elevated decay curves in `pre_flight.py`.
- **Dynamic Risk/Reward & Stop Loss (Issue #2 & #4)**: Bound target setting and stop loss derivation to an IV curve, expanding the safety buffer against high-noise market environments while hardening a `2.0` R:R minimum floor.

## Analytics & Screener Fixes
- **DTE-Aware Screener Efficiency (Issue #3)**: Eliminated hardcoded `0.10` S-score capping, switching to a dynamically computed `eff_cap` (relaxing efficiency ceilings for near-expiry options where natural theta/delta ratios breach previous constant parameters).
- **Directional Candidate Pools (Issue #5)**: Passed independent `direction=direction` queries directly into `get_strikes()` to ensure Calls and Puts no longer cannibalize the same 20 `SCREENER_TOP_N` pool slots.
- **Expiry Tier Isolation (Issue #6)**: Restricted the underlying S-score queries in `screener.py` via `AND o.expiry_tier IN ('TIER1', 'TIER2')`, preventing monthly `TIER3` contracts with incompatible surface topologies from leaking into live recommendations. 

## Secondary Quality & Formatting Updates
- **Normalized S-score Caps (Issue #7)**: Redefined `_SSCORE_MAX` and `.80` norm baselines in `quality.py` to match the exact `W_*` momentum weights used within the SQL query, correcting Grade calculations.
- **Unused Pre-flight Session Param Removed (Issue #8)**: Deprecated the `session` keyword argument loop into the evaluation signature to clear tech debt.
- **Quality Base Block (Issue #9)**: Enforced a `PREFLIGHT_MIN_QUALITY_SCORE=50` (Grade C) checkpoint in `recommender.py` before finalizing live journal pushes.
- **Signal-to-Narrative Piping (Issue #10)**: Bound `conviction` (STRING), `veto` (STRING), and `pcr_modifier` to the live `build_narrative()` output to visually explain to the user why the Recommender chose structural downgrades.
