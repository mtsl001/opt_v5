# OptDash v2.9.2 (Directional Bias Engine Fixes)

This release addresses critical logic and threshold issues in the Directional Bias Engine, strengthening conviction validation, structurally aligning gates, and resolving correlation artifacts.

## Core Engine Fixes (`direction.py`)
- **Minimum Margin Threshold (Issue #1)**: Implemented a hard floor (`MIN_DIRECTION_MARGIN = 3`) to block low-conviction/solitary signals (like ATM OBI alone) from triggering a live directional bias, enforcing a stricter standard before moving ahead.
- **PCR Sentiment Reform (Issue #2)**: PCR divergence was removed from the additive signal vote pool (since it's a contrarian retail indicator) and transformed into a post-vote confidence multiplier.
- **VEX Structural Veto (Issue #3)**: VEX opposition now functions as a hard structural veto against trades fighting dealer hedging obligations, capping the trade rather than marginally diluting confidence votes.
- **V_CoC Spike Decay (Issue #4)**: Replaced boolean V_CoC spike tracking with a time-decaying weight multiplier, reducing the impact of aging spikes.
- **De-Correlated Institutional Signals (Issue #5)**: Combined V_CoC and Futures OBI signal capping (max 4 joint weight) synchronizes the engine with the `environment.py` Gate to prevent double-counting institutional momentum.
- **Conviction Tiers (Issue #6)**: Introduced an explicit `conviction` string (STRONG/MODERATE/WEAK) returned directly from the engine to support dynamic narrative styling.

## Downstream Fixes (`confidence.py`, `environment.py`, `pre_flight.py`)
- **Confidence Diversity (Issue #7)**: Switched the base formula in `compute_confidence()` Bucket 1 to use `unique_source_count` rather than raw signal count to accurately cap confidence scores from correlated signal pairs.
- **Gate Terminology Clarification (Issue #8)**: Renamed the C6 environment gate key from `obi_negative` to `obi_significant` to reflect its bidirectional nature.
- **Elevated DTE=0/1 Guard (Issue #9)**: Added a margin-floor requirement (`PREFLIGHT_DTE1_MIN_MARGIN`) to `pre_flight.py` Rule 7, stopping WEAK-conviction trades from executing on highly volatile expiry days.
