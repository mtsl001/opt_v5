# Release Notes — v2.6.0 (VEX/CEX & IV Analytics Reliability - Part A)

**Date:** 2026-03-22  
**Type:** Core Analytics & AI Model Upgrade  
**Branch:** `fix/vex-iv-analytics-part-a`  

This release represents a major structural upgrade to the Implied Volatility (IV) and Dealer Hedging (VEX/CEX) analytical modules. By moving away from mathematical heuristics to exact Black-Scholes partial derivatives, and by deeply integrating Volatility Risk Premium (VRP) into the AI Confidence Model, the signals generated by OptDash are now significantly more robust to the nuances of retail options clustering and high-IV events.

---

## 🏗️ Core Structural Mechanics

### 1. Exact BSM Vanna & Charm (VEX-1 & VEX-2)
The legacy approximations for Vanna (`δ × (1 - |δ|) / (spot × σ × √T)`) and Charm (`-θ / (spot × σ × √T)`) acted as acceptable proxies near-ATM but systematically degraded for ITM and OTM strikes. Because retail option activity clusters heavily in OTM wings, relying on theta as a proxy for charm conflated pure time-value decay with the decay of delta-sensitivity, generating inaccurate Dealer exposures (VEX/CEX).

* **Upgrade:** The `processor.py` extraction pipeline now computes exact Black-Scholes partial derivatives for Vanna and Charm natively on vectorized arrays.
* **New Base Mechanics:** 
  * `Vanna = -(Vega_BSM × d2) / (Spot × σ × √T)`
  * `Charm = -N'(d1) × [2rT - d2 × σ√T] / [2T × σ√T]`
* **Dependencies & Config:** Introduced exact RBI repo-rate linkage (`RISK_FREE_RATE = 0.0625`) and added `scipy` dependency for highly performant, vectorized normal distribution mapping over thousands of rows per snap.
* **⚠️ Breaking Change:** The mathematical accuracy means historical VEX/CEX Parquets are logically incompatible. Deployment requires full `data/processed/` purge and waterfowl backfill to guarantee signal continuity.

### 2. GEX-Conditional Charm Direction (VEX-5)
The previous logic dynamically flagged heavy Charm flow, but assumed an absolute bullish drift. This assumption ignored the reality of market-maker delta neutralization: whether dealers buy or sell the underlying as Charm decays depends on whether they are net *long* or *short* options overall. 
* **Upgrade:** The `_interpret()` module inside `vex_cex.py` now queries total market GEX. 
* **New Logic:** If dealers are short options (GEX < 0), Charm decay mandates dealer *buying* (Bullish Support). If dealers are long options (GEX > 0), they are forced into *selling* (Bearish Drift). Dealer O'Clock pinning logic is now fully conditional on this polarity.

## 📈 IV & AI Confidence Model Enhancements

### 3. Volatility Risk Premium into Structural Quality (IV-OPEN-2)
Option premiums consistently overstate realized volatility, acting as a structural edge for sellers. However, when Implied Volatility systematically drops *below* 20-day Realised Volatility (HV20), the Volatility Risk Premium (`VRP`) goes negative (`UNDERPRICED` regime). 
* **Upgrade:** Statistically, this represents the strongest mathematical entry context for option buyers. The `ai/confidence.py` engine now extracts the `vrp_regime` and grants a targeted **+3 points bonus** into the Structural Quality bucket when options are definitively underpriced.

### 4. Config Exposures & Sanity Tracking (IV-OPEN-1, IV-OPEN-3)
Ensured all dynamic IV bounds operate transparently via tunable environment logic.
* Migrated previously hardcoded bounds to `config.py` constants (`VRP_OVERPRICED_THRESHOLD = 2.0`, `VIX_HIGH_THRESHOLD = 20.0`, `VIX_HIGH_IVP_THRESHOLD = 35.0`).
* Protected against config collision via Pydantic model validation on the VRP underpricing boundary checks.

## 🛡️ Calibration & Data Quality Protection

### 5. Cross-Underlying VEX Calibration (VEX-4)
The VEX signal thresholds were fixed at `0.50` (Rs M) globally. However, `BANKNIFTY` trades at approximately 43% of the notional value per lot compared to `NIFTY` (Spot × 15 vs Spot × 75). Consequently, VEX signals for the banking constituent were systematically under-triggering. 
* **Upgrade:** Tuned `BANKNIFTY` magnitude thresholds strictly proportional to total notional limit down to `0.35` to guarantee signal parity.

### 6. Pipeline Feed Quality Gateways (VEX-3)
Near-zero IV readings coming natively from the NSE upstream feed can drastically impact denominator math in VEX/CEX evaluation, causing extreme values that historically clipped silently.
* **Upgrade:** The `processor.py` vectorization logic now evaluates the un-clipped raw array dimensions. A dynamic warning event now fires whenever more than `5%` of an underlying's options chain hits the structural `VANNA_CLIP` or `CHARM_CLIP` limits. This cleanly separates legitimate market dynamics from corrupt BQ feed data.
