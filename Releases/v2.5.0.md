# Release Notes — v2.5.0

**Date:** 2026-03-21  
**Type:** IV Analytics Overhaul (IV-1 through IV-6)

---

## Summary
Complete recalibration of the Implied Volatility (IV) module. The engine now safely filters historical extremes, applies DTE-normalized modeling for term structure, and dynamically bridges the `upxtx_vix` side-channel feed directly into the central Environment Gate parameters.

---

## What's New & Changed

### 1. `IV-1`: HV20 Intraday Close Resolution
- Switched the historical log-return calculation from using the intraday `MAX(spot)` to using the explicit `LAST(spot ORDER BY snap_time)`.
- **Reasoning:** Intraday highs artificially expand the realized volatility baseline, skewing output higher on strong trending days. The new query anchors cleanly to the 15:29/15:30 closing print metric.

### 2. `IV-2`: IVR Percentile Capping
- Substituted hardcoded `MIN()` and `MAX()` extremes in the 252-day IV window with dynamic 1st and 99th percentile limitations (`PERCENTILE_CONT(0.01 / 0.99)`).
- **Reasoning:** A singular black swan outlier (e.g. general election day) permanently anchored IV-High, resulting in 200+ days of artificially suppressed IVR readings. 

### 3. `IV-3`: Term Structure Slope Normalization
- Obsoleted the fixed `< 0.95 / > 1.05` near-to-far expiry ratio.
- Implemented `(IV_far - IV_near) / (√DTE_far - √DTE_near)`. 
- **Reasoning:** Normal calendar carry mathematically expands static ratios leading into expiration week. Translating the differential to mathematically uniform slope constants (`± 0.30`) suppresses false term-structure warnings in Gate C7.

### 4. `IV-4 / IV-5`: VRP Naming & Safe Type Enforcement
- Transformed the ambiguous `iv_hv_spread` terminology to proper `vrp` (Volatility Risk Premium).
- Added `vrp_regime` tagging (`OVERPRICED`, `UNDERPRICED`, `FAIR`).
- Hardened the `hv20` fallbacks — previously, `(None or atm_iv)` returned 0.0 spread, masking insufficient history. The payload now accurately yields `None`.

### 5. `IV-6`: India VIX Cross-Channel Orchestration
- `iv.py` natively acquires dynamic macro-volatility readings directly via the distinct `data/processed/vix/` Parquet sidecar without interrupting primary options ingestion.
- Gate C5 (`ivp_cheap`) actively penalizes trades when `india_vix` thresholds breach `20.0`. During elevated regimes, options must mathematically fall below the `35th` percentile (rather than the `50th` percentile) to score a 'cheap' rating point.
