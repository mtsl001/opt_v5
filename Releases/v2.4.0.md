# Release Notes — v2.4.0

**Date:** 2026-03-21  
**Type:** PCR Analytics Overhaul (PCR-1 through PCR-4)

---

## Summary

Complete modernization of the Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Divergence analytics engine. This release abandons static thresholds in favor of self-calibrating rolling intraday Z-scores, adds protection against expiry-day gamma distortions, and introduces short-term trend awareness.

---

## What's New & Changed

### 1. `PCR-1`: Configured Asymmetric Thresholds
- Documented the exact rationale behind using asymmetric thresholds (`PCR_DIV_BULL_THRESHOLD` = 0.25 vs `PCR_DIV_BEAR_THRESHOLD` = -0.20) in `config.py`.
- **Reasoning:** Retail panic-buying of puts (driven by fear) triggers sharper anomalies than retail call-buying (driven by fomo/greed), requiring differently scaled confirmation gates.
- Added `PCR_ZSCORE_WINDOW=30` and `PCR_TREND_SNAPS=3` to `.env`/config.

### 2. `PCR-2`: TIER2 Near-Expiry Distortion Protection
- The entire PCR calculation engine (`get_pcr()` and `get_pcr_series()`) now queries both `TIER1` (nearest) and `TIER2` (second-nearest) expiries concurrently via single-pass SQL.
- When `DTE <= 1`, the system automatically shifts its primary `pcr_divergence` metric from `TIER1` to `TIER2`. This protects the directional signal from the aggressive, localized gamma scalping distortions common on expiry day. 
- Integrated `tier_used` tracking explicitly into the payload.
- Additionally, updated the `_smoothed_obi` dependency inside `pcr.py` to utilize Level-1 instantaneous depth rather than cumulative volume, mirroring the recent CoC-3 logic update.

### 3. `PCR-3`: Dynamic Rolling Intraday Z-Score
- Upgraded the analytical pipeline to use a 30-period rolling standardization rather than responding solely to absolute float divergences.
- Employed `AVG` and `STDDEV_SAMP` window functions inside DuckDB's `get_pcr_series()` query to extract the historical mean and deviation dynamically.
- `_pcr_signal()` transitioned to `_pcr_signal_z()`, allowing the engine to fire `RETAIL_PANIC_PUTS` or `RETAIL_PANIC_CALLS` effectively on relative volatility spikes (`|Z| > 1.5`) across both high and low beta underlyings.

### 4. `PCR-4`: Divergence Trend Detection
- Evaluates the `current_div` comparatively against an `N=3` historical lag.
- If the divergence crosses the absolute or Z-score PANIC threshold, but its intraday momentum reflects strong reversion (e.g. shrinking toward zero by `0.05`), the signal output is purposely stunted to `DIVERGENCE_FADING` to prevent false entries.

### 5. Environment Gate C4 (ENV) Annotation Injection
- Modified the main `get_environment_score` entrypoint in `environment.py`. 
- Gate C4 now formally detects and tags the specific underlying expiration (`"TIER1"` vs `"TIER2"`) driving the PCR signal on Expiry days. This completely eliminates silent logic swaps.
