# Release v2.8.0 — Strike Screener & S_Score Algorithmic Overhaul

This release introduces significant structural and mathematical improvements to the `S_score` strike screener. The updates address specific algorithmic inefficiencies and execution risks identified in internal and external reviews, resulting in a more regime-aware and execution-safe screener.

## 🚀 Core Engine Changes (Screener Logic)

### 1. Delta Normalization (Fix for Delta Weight Asymmetry)
- **Previous:** `W_DELTA × ABS(delta)` directly applied, clipping delta's effective weight relative to `W_EFF_RATIO`.
- **New:** Delta is now normalized to its filtered range: `(ABS(delta) - MIN_DELTA) / (MAX_DELTA - MIN_DELTA)`.
- **Impact:** Scales delta’s contribution accurately from $0$–$1$, fully restoring its intended 4.0 weight in the `S_score` and keeping directional options competitive.

### 2. Theta-to-Delta Efficiency Ratio (Fix for Theta Double-Counting)
- **Previous:** Theta was heavily double-penalized through both `W_THETA` and `W_EFF_RATIO` against raw LTP, creating a structural bias against 0-DTE.
- **New:** Replaced the dual penalty with a single **Theta-to-Delta Ratio** (`|theta| / delta`) mapped to the `W_EFF_RATIO` factor.
- **Impact:** Measures how much time decay you pay *per unit of directional exposure*, correctly normalizing efficiency across all expirations without hostile 0-DTE bias. The redundant `W_THETA` logic has been streamlined out of the formula.

### 3. ITM Option Inclusion (Expanded Delta Filter)
- **Previous:** Delta filter arbitrarily capped at `0.10 - 0.50`, excluding all ITM options.
- **New:** Filter boundaries expanded to `0.20 - 0.65` via `config.py`.
- **Impact:** Captures ITM options ($0.50 - 0.65$) that carry higher intrinsic value, lower relative theta decay, and behave more like the underlying futures for directional plays.

### 4. Regime-Aware IV Penalty
- **Previous:** Constant penalty for high raw IV, making structurally sound trades artificially low-ranked in high-volatility regimes.
- **New:** The `$100`-cap IV penalty is now **gated** and activates only when IV Percentile (`IVP`) is $< 50$.
- **Impact:** Automatically stops penalizing high absolute IV when the global IV regime is elevated, preventing false negatives on sound entries.

### 5. Slippage Penalty via Bid-Ask Spread
- **Previous:** Liquidity scored purely on absolute Open Interest $\times$ Last Traded Price, ignoring actual spread width.
- **New:** Injected a `spread_penalty` multiplier (`1 - LEAST(1, (ask - bid) / (ltp * 0.05))`) directly into the liquidity score component.
- **Impact:** Options with wide bid-ask gaps are aggressively penalized. Eliminates recommendations that look good on paper but incur immediate $5\%$+ slippage costs on actual entry/exit.

### 6. Dynamic Liquidity Caps
- **Previous:** Static `₹5 Cr` notional liquidity cap across all indices.
- **New:** Introduced parameterised per-underlying caps (`LIQUIDITY_CAP_CR`).
- **Impact:** Properly normalizes heavily traded indices. Examples: `BANKNIFTY` now caps at ₹25 Cr, while `MIDCPNIFTY` caps at ₹5 Cr, ensuring liquidity scores are meaningful relative to the index's baseline depth.

### 7. Volume Momentum Factor
- **Previous:** Volume was entirely ignored in strike selection.
- **New:** Added `volume / avg_volume_20d` as an 8th scoring factor, capped at $3\times$ and weighted by `W_MOMENTUM = 1.0`.
- **Impact:** Strikes experiencing abnormal volume surges are explicitly rewarded as real-world indicators of informed interest.

***

**Theoretical S_score Maximum Adjustments:**
Due to delta normalization and the momentum additions, the theoretical maximum `S_score` scales appropriately up to ~180. The 4-star, 3-star, and 2-star threshold ratings gracefully handle the updated composite values constraint-free.
